## Prefixes

A leading number followed by `-`, `_` or `.` sets order and is removed from the
slug. `02-install.mdx` is served at `/install` and sorts after `01-naming.mdx`.

## Ordering strategies

Three strategies are available, set per folder in `config.yml`:

- `prefix` orders by the numeric prefix, falling back to the title
- `alphabetical` orders by title, comparing numbers inside it naturally
- `timestamp` orders by the `date` in each page's frontmatter

`timestamp` defaults to newest first; the others default to ascending. Set
`direction` to override.

## Hiding a folder

Give it `hidden: true` in its `config.yml` and it disappears from the tree
without being deleted.
